What you're seeing

Every match gets a probability for each outcome. The model turns the win/draw/loss chances into expected goals for both teams, then works out all the other markets from those goals: Double Chance, Draw No Bet, Over/Under, GG/NG and Correct Score.

Fair odds

Fair odds are 1 divided by the probability. If a bookmaker's odds are higher than the fair odds, the bet has value. If they're lower, it doesn't.
